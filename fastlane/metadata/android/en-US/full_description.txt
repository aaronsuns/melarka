Melarka is the Android app for your own Melarka music server. It needs a Melarka server that you run yourself (see github.com/aaronsuns/melarka); the app does not include any music.

The app shows your server's web app full screen and plays the audio with a native player, which gives it what a browser tab on a phone cannot do reliably:

- Playback that keeps going with the screen off, with lock-screen, notification, headphone and car (Bluetooth) controls
- Gapless transitions, with the next songs downloaded ahead
- An offline cache of your favorites, synced on Wi-Fi, with a size limit you choose
- Launcher shortcuts: Shuffle Favorites and Resume
- Car autoplay: your favorites start by themselves when the phone connects to your car
- The current lyric line on the lock screen and the car's display
- Works with servers behind your own certificate authority, once you have installed its certificate on the phone

Your server needs HTTPS (plain http works only for localhost and .local addresses). Songs found through YouTube search are downloaded by your server; the app only plays what your server has.
