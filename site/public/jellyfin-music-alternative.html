A Jellyfin music alternative built only for music

Jellyfin is a free media system for video, music and photos. If your goal is a TV and movie library, it is hard to beat. If what you want is a Spotify-like experience on phones, a server that does only music can be simpler. That is the gap Melarka tries to fill.

Where Jellyfin is better

  • Everything in one place. Movies, shows, live TV, photos and music, with apps for TVs, game consoles and streaming boxes. Melarka does only music (and YouTube video and channels through your server).
  • Big ecosystem. A large community, many clients, plugins and years of documentation.
  • Several music apps. Besides Jellyfin's own clients, there are dedicated third-party music apps that connect to a Jellyfin server.

Where Melarka fits

Jellyfin's music support is one feature of a large product. Melarka is built around music at home and around the phone:

  • Apps made for music. The Melarka iPhone, Android and web apps are the product, not an add-on. Background play, gapless transitions, an offline cache of favorites, a sleep timer, volume normalization and a queue you edit by hand.
  • Per-person experience. Each family member has their own favorites, playlists, history, radio and "For you" picks.
  • Songs from YouTube. Search and download songs or playlists straight from the app, or follow YouTube channels without an account.
  • Lyrics. Synced lyrics from the file, a .lrc file or online sources, with a way for anyone to fix timing.
  • Car autoplay. Favorites start when the phone connects to the car's Bluetooth (Android setting; Shortcuts on iPhone).

Using Jellyfin for video and Melarka for music

You do not have to pick one. A common setup is Jellyfin for movies and shows on the living-room TV, and Melarka for the family's music on phones and in the car. They are separate Docker services; both can live on the same NAS and read different folders. Melarka needs little: it runs on a small home server or a Raspberry Pi-class machine, and transcoding uses ffmpeg at low priority.

What you give up

Melarka has no TV app and no video library for your own movies. Its iPhone app is installed with your own Apple ID rather than from the App Store. If your family listens mostly on a TV box, Jellyfin or Plex will serve you better. Read the Navidrome comparison too if a music-only server is what you want but Subsonic apps matter to you.

FAQ

Is Melarka a replacement for Jellyfin?

Only for music. Melarka has no movie or TV library and no TV apps. Many people run Jellyfin for video and Melarka for music.

Does Melarka play video?

It can search and play YouTube videos through your server, and follow YouTube channels. It does not manage a library of your own films.

Can both use the same music folder?

Yes. Melarka only reads the folder, so another server can share it.

What does Melarka need to run?

A Linux machine or NAS with Docker (amd64 or arm64). A small home server is enough for a household.
