Your family's own Spotify, on your own server.

给全家用的音乐 App，完美替代 Spotify。

Melarka is a self-hosted music server. Songs come from YouTube or your own music folders; everyone at home gets their own favorites, playlists, radio and synced lyrics, with native iPhone and Android apps. Free and open source.

Melarka 是装在自己家里的音乐服务器。歌来自 YouTube 或你自己的本地音乐；全家每人一个账号，各自的收藏、歌单、私人电台和同步歌词，iPhone、安卓都有原生 App。开源免费。

Why I built it

为什么做这个

I wanted Spotify-style listening for my family, on music we keep ourselves and without a subscription. So I built Melarka, and I use it every day.

On the way to work, the phone connects to the car's Bluetooth and my favorites start playing by themselves, with the lyric line on the car's screen. On walks, the favorites are already on the phone, so no signal is no problem. At home, everyone has their own account, and a song one of us adds is there for the whole family.

It has improved a lot from daily use. Try it, and tell me what you'd change.

我想让全家像用 Spotify 一样听歌，但听的是我们自己的音乐，也不用交会员费。于是做了 Melarka，现在每天都在用。

上下班开车，手机一连上车载蓝牙，收藏的歌自动开始放，车机屏幕上显示歌词。出去散步，收藏的歌早就缓存在手机里，没信号也照样听。在家里，每人一个账号，谁下载的好歌，全家都能听到。

天天用，一直在改进，好用得很。欢迎试用，提提意见。

Install in one command

一条命令安装

On a Linux machine (or NAS) with Docker. Put your music folder at the end, or leave it out and add songs later:

在装了 Docker 的 Linux 电脑或 NAS 上运行。命令最后加上你的音乐文件夹路径；不加也行，之后再放歌：

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aaronsuns/melarka/main/deploy/install.sh | sh -s -- /path/to/your/music
  1. Open the address it prints and sign in as admin with the password it prints.
  2. Add your family under Admin → Users.
  3. For phones, put HTTPS in front of it, then install the apps.
  1. 打开它显示的地址，用 admin 和它显示的密码登录。
  2. 在 管理 → 用户 里给家人建账号。
  3. 手机上用之前，先给它配好 HTTPS，再装 App。

Questions

常见问题

Is it free?

Yes, free and open source. You run it on your own machine; there is no account with us and nothing to pay.

要钱吗？

不要，开源免费。装在你自己的机器上，不需要在我们这里注册，也没有任何收费。

What do I need?

A Linux machine or NAS with Docker that stays on, and, for phones, an HTTPS address for it (a reverse proxy; the README shows how).

需要什么？

一台一直开着、装了 Docker 的 Linux 电脑或 NAS；手机上用还需要给它配一个 HTTPS 地址（反向代理，README 里有步骤）。

Why isn't the iPhone app in the App Store?

It is a personal open-source project. SideStore signs it on your phone with your own Apple ID; a free one is enough.

iPhone App 为什么不在 App Store？

这是个人开源项目。SideStore 用你自己的 Apple ID 在手机上签名安装，免费的 Apple ID 就够了。

Does it change my music files?

Never. It only reads them. Titles, tags and lyrics you edit are stored in Melarka's own database.

会改我的音乐文件吗？

不会，只读取。你改的歌名、标签、歌词都存在 Melarka 自己的数据库里。
